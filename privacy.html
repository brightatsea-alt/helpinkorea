Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

helpinKorea ("we") collects only the information you submit through the inquiry form or by email: your name, email address, country, optional phone number, company details and the description of your request.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote and, if you engage us, deliver the service. We do not send newsletters or marketing emails and we do not sell or share your data with third parties, except service providers strictly needed to deliver email (form and email hosting) or to perform a service you have engaged us for (for example a licensed attorney or customs broker), each bound by confidentiality.

Retention

Inquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for the period required by Korean tax and commercial law.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data by emailing tailwind@kakao.com.

Cookies

The site uses only strictly necessary cookies and, where enabled, privacy-respecting analytics to count visits.
